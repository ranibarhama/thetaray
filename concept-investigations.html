A workspace analysts shape themselves (if they want…)

Nothing is fixed and nothing hides the case: every section is a floating card — move it, pin evidence to the desk, arrange the screen the way you work — and jump back to the default view in one click. The same floating approach also makes the product easy to embed in a customer's environment — cards can live inside existing screens and workflows.
